                        Settings

                        Profile

                        Picture

                        Shown to everybody on this server, cropped to a circle. Any image works; it is shrunk to a small square before it is uploaded. Without one, your initials are drawn instead.

                        Banner

                        Drawn behind your name in the member list and in voice channels, dimmed and fading out toward the left. A picture or a GIF, up to 2 MB; a still picture is shrunk to fit.

                        Shown to everybody instead of your username. Capitals, spaces and accents are all fine, and two people can pick the same one. Empty goes back to your username.

                        Name font

                        How your display name is drawn for everybody on this server. The fonts come with the app, so everybody sees the same one.

                        Name colour

                        The colour of your display name for everybody on this server. It replaces the colour of your role.

                        Join sound

                        Played for the people in a voice channel when you join it, instead of the usual tone. Up to 10 seconds: anything longer is cut at 10. Any audio file your computer can play works.

                        Account
                        Username
                        Role

                        Your username is what you sign in with and what people @mention. It does not change.

                        Security

                        Password

                        Change the password you sign in with. Any other device signed in to this account is signed out when you do.

                        Password recovery

                        Write three questions only you can answer. If you forget your password you can choose a new one from the sign-in screen by answering all three.

                        Answers do not care about capital letters, accents, punctuation or extra spaces. They are stored scrambled, like a password, and cannot be read back; saving replaces all three.

                        Appearance

                        The language of the menus and messages in this app. People on the same server can each use their own; what is written in a chat stays as it was written.

                        Colour palette

                        Yours alone. Nothing is sent to the server, and two people in the same channel can be looking at different colours.
